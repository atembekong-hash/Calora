Decision rule

Do not demand theoretical perfection.

A GO requires no unresolved Critical or High issue that could cause:

unauthorized Fact Context egress;

cross-account leakage;

broad legacy-context leakage;

consent bypass;

inability to stop rollout promptly;

unsafe provider claims;

production deployment ambiguity.


Medium/Low findings may be accepted only if clearly documented, bounded and not material to the proposed first cohort.

Deferred physical-device/accessibility checks remain deferred and must not be falsely marked passed.
