Any discovered issue must be classified:

P0 — release-stopping/security/data-loss/crash/auth failure
P1 — major functional failure or major production regression
P2 — important but non-blocking defect
P3 — cosmetic/minor issue
OBSERVATION — not confirmed defect
OWNER DEVICE TEST REQUIRED — cannot be independently verified by Replit

For every defect include:

- ID;
- severity;
- area;
- exact symptom;
- reproduction path;
- affected files/components/routes;
- evidence;
- likely root cause;
- whether deterministic;
- release impact;
- recommended remediation;
- regression test needed.
