# Calora Learning editorial-photo sources

These images are locally bundled editorial artwork for the Calora Learning library. They are not fetched at runtime and do not send user data to a third party.

The files were collected from Unsplash search results on 2026-10-28 for healthy meals, wellness movement, and coffee/tea morning still life. Unsplash’s license states that images may be downloaded and used for commercial and non-commercial purposes without permission; attribution is appreciated. Source: https://unsplash.com/license

- `balanced-meal-unsplash-mobile.jpg` — Healthy Meals search result, Unsplash.
- `coffee-morning-unsplash-mobile.jpg` — Coffee Morning search result, Unsplash.
- `tea-journal-unsplash-mobile.jpg` — Morning Tea search result, Unsplash.
- `grain-bowl-unsplash-mobile.jpg` — Balanced Meal search result, Unsplash.
- `colorful-plate-unsplash-mobile.jpg` — Balanced Meal search result, Unsplash.
- `wellness-yoga-unsplash-mobile.jpg` — Health and Wellness search result, Unsplash.
- `outdoor-movement-unsplash-mobile.jpg` — Health Woman search result, Unsplash.

The original local staging copies were deliberately excluded; only optimized mobile JPEG files are shipped.
