# Learning five-per-topic editorial source ledger

**Author:** Manus AI
**Review date:** 2026-10-28

The expanded Calora Learning library contains 50 general-education lessons, with five lessons in each canonical topic. The topics below were reviewed against the cited public-health sources. Content avoids universal targets, diagnostic claims, and promised outcomes. Medical-adjacent lessons retain explicit qualified-professional referral and safety boundaries.

## Topics reviewed

**Fundamentals.** The new lessons use flexible variety, everyday movement, and protecting a sleep opportunity rather than food rules or idealized routines. [1] [2] [3]

**Planning and Habits.** The new lessons emphasize small, specific actions, stable cues, lower-friction backups, and nonjudgmental review instead of streaks or moralized compliance. [4] [5] [6]

**Recipes and Labels.** The new lessons use flexible meal structures, small recipe adjustments, safe prep and leftover handling, serving context, and focused ingredient or allergen reading. [7] [8] [9] [10]

**Fasting.** The new lessons define meal timing as optional, avoid restrictive challenges, and direct people with relevant health, medication, pregnancy, breastfeeding, age, or eating-concern factors to qualified individual guidance. [11] [12] [13]

**Hydration.** The new lessons use access and context-aware check-ins rather than a universal fluid quota, and they identify serious or persistent symptoms as reasons to seek appropriate help. [14] [15] [16]

**Movement.** The new lessons make room for accessible short movement options and flexible weekly planning, with gradual progression and symptom-stop guidance. [17] [18]

**Sleep.** The new lessons use realistic routine cues, a supportive sleep environment, and immediate drowsiness-safety boundaries without presenting habit changes as treatment. [19] [20]

**Mindset.** The new lessons use nonjudgmental observation, a small next step, and an explicit instruction to stop or seek support if a reflection practice increases distress. [21] [22]

## Safeguards retained

The app makes no external runtime image requests for Learning or the primary More destinations. Editorial photography is statically bundled, governance-registered, and decorative; screen labels and article copy remain accessible content. There are no tracking, advertising, signed-URL, or remote-photo surfaces in the modified areas.
